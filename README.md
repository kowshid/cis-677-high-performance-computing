# CIS 660 - Data Engineering

This repository contains course contents, and assignments for **CIS 677: High-performance Computing**.

## Course Overview

High-performance computing used to mean writing fast code. It increasingly means deciding what to make fast, directing
tools to do it, and proving that they did, without any other regressions. An agent will produce many optimized variants
of your kernel in a minute, but it will not tell you whether that was the right thing to do on your system in your
environment for your purposes, or what it may have broken that was more important than the faster kernel. That judgment
is the subject of this course.
